Our Services

Yoga is an ancient art that connects the mind and body. It is an exercise that we perform by balancing the elements of our bodies. In addition, it helps us meditate and relax. Moreover, yoga helps us keep control of our bodies as well as mind. It is a great channel for releasing our stress and anxiety.

General Yoga Classes

Hatha Yoga

Hatha Yoga is a branch of yoga that emphasizes physical exercises to master the body along with mind exercises to withdraw it from external objects.

Power Yoga

Power Yoga is a fitness-based vinyasa practice. It has many of the same qualities and benefits, including building internal heat, increased stamina, strength, and flexibility.

Vinyasa Yoga

Vinyasa Yoga is characterized by stringing postures together so that you move from one to another, seamlessly, using breath.

Ashtanga Yoga

Ashtanga Yoga involves synchronizing the breath with a progressive series of postures—a process producing intense internal heat and a profuse, purifying sweat.

Bikram Yoga

Bikram Yoga is a system of hot yoga, a type of yoga as exercise, devised by Bikram Choudhury and based on traditional hatha yoga techniques.

Pragya Yoga

Pragya Yoga integrates the ancient spiritual knowledge with contemporary scientific thought, focusing on mental well-being and consciousness.

Stick Yoga

Stick Yoga uses a stick as a prop for postural alignments and deeper stretches, enhancing the physical benefits of yoga poses.

Belt Yoga

Belt Yoga incorporates belts to aid in stretching, achieving correct postures, and increasing flexibility and strength.

Iyengar Yoga

Iyengar Yoga is a form of Hatha Yoga that emphasizes detail, precision, and alignment in the performance of posture and breath control.

Chair Yoga

Chair Yoga is a gentle form of yoga that is practiced sitting on a chair or standing using a chair for support, suitable for people with limited mobility.

Ball Yoga

Ball Yoga uses an exercise ball to enhance balance, flexibility, and core strength through various yoga poses and movements.

Advance Practice

Advance Practice involves more complex poses and sequences, aimed at experienced practitioners to deepen their yoga practice.

All Age Group

Our yoga classes cater to all age groups, ensuring a suitable and safe practice for everyone, from children to seniors.

Pranayama and Shatkarma

Pranayama

Pranayama involves breath control exercises that help in enhancing the respiratory system and calming the mind.

Shatkarma (Jal Neti, Rabber Neti, Kunjal)

Shatkarma refers to yogic cleansing techniques such as Jal Neti, Rabber Neti, and Kunjal that purify the body and prepare it for higher yoga practices.

Special Services

Pregnancy Yoga

Pregnancy Yoga is tailored to the needs of pregnant women, helping them maintain fitness, reduce stress, and prepare for childbirth.

Yoga for Students

Yoga for Students focuses on improving concentration, reducing stress, and promoting physical and mental well-being among students.

Yoga for Senior Citizens

Yoga for Senior Citizens offers gentle and accessible yoga practices that improve mobility, balance, and overall health for seniors.

Personal/Home Classes

Personal/Home Classes provide one-on-one yoga sessions tailored to individual needs, either at the yoga center or at your home.

Meditation

Meditation involves mindfulness and concentration techniques that promote mental clarity, relaxation, and overall well-being.
